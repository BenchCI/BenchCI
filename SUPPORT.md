# BenchCI Support

Support is delivered directly by engineers who build BenchCI. Commitments below are
response times, not resolution times.

| Plan | Support channel | Response commitment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local (free) | Docs + [public issue tracker](../../issues) | Best effort |
| Starter | Email — tech@benchci.dev | 2 business days |
| Team | Priority email — tech@benchci.dev | Next business day |
| Enterprise | Per agreement | Negotiated |

New Team workspaces also include a free 30-minute onboarding call — reply to your welcome
email to schedule it.

## Where to go for what

- **"How do I…?"** — start with the [documentation](https://docs.benchci.dev) and the
  [`examples/`](examples/) directory; the [FAQ](docs/faq.md) covers common setup questions.
- **Bug reports and feature requests** — open a [GitHub issue](../../issues/new/choose).
  Anyone may file issues, on any plan.
- **Account, billing, or security-sensitive topics** — email tech@benchci.dev. Never put
  tokens, firmware, or customer data in a public issue.
- **Vulnerability reports** — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Good bug reports

Include: `benchci --version`, OS, the command you ran, expected vs. actual behavior, and —
if it is bench-related — the output of `benchci doctor`. Strip serial numbers and tokens
before posting logs.
